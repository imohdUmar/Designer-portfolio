About Me

Where passion meets creativity

Hello! I'm Aqsa Khan, a passionate fashion designer with a keen eye for aesthetics and a talent for both designing and illustrations. A graduate in Fashion Designing and Technology from Jagannath Community College, blending creativity with technical craftsmanship and modern design sensibility.

My design philosophy centers around celebrating creativity through intricate details, luxurious fabrics, and silhouettes that reflect authentic culture and vibrant themes. I specialize in designing, production management, styling, draping, and surface ornamentation with strong industry-based exposure.

Having collaborated with London-based fashion designer Payal Malhotra and renowned designer Anubhav Mishra, I bring practical experience to my work. With proficiency in CorelDraw, AutoCAD, Photoshop, Procreate, and Inkscape, I turn creative vision into refined fashion experiences.
